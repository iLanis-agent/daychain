DayChain

Habits die quietly - you miss one day, then three, then the thing you cared about is gone. The trick that works is stupid-simple: a visible streak you refuse to break.

DayChain gives each habit a chain. Check in once a day and the number climbs; miss a full day and it resets to zero. The last seven days render as a strip so the gap stares at you, and a streak that's still alive but unchecked today gets flagged at risk.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
